Deep Time: Idle Excavation Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Who is responsible

Deep Time: Idle Excavation ("the game") is developed and published by Joan Marc Sanahuja ("we", "us"), an individual resident in Spain. For any question about this policy or your data, write to jmssdeveloper@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • The game does not collect personal data. It has no accounts of its own, no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting and no in-app purchases, and it does not track you across apps or websites.
  • Your save is stored only on your device. We never receive it and cannot see it.
  • The game does not connect to any server of ours. All content (graphics, sounds, text and fonts) ships inside the app.
  • If you are signed in to Game Center (iOS/iPadOS) or Google Play Games (Android), the game reports the achievements you earn to them. These are Apple and Google services and follow their own privacy policies.
  • We only handle personal data in two cases: if you email us and, on Android, the limited order details Google Play shows us when you buy the game (section 6).

3. What is stored on your device

So that you can play, the game stores the following in the app's private storage:

WhatWhy
Your save: progress, finds, museum, upgrades, achievements earned, gameplay statistics (for example, time played) and settings (language, haptics, interface size, accessibility and sound options)To continue where you left off
The date and time you last played (inside the save)To work out what your museum produced while you were away
A backup of your previous saveSo you can undo an import or recover a damaged save
Interface preferences: chosen language, preferred view of some screens and which help tips you have already seenSo the game doesn't repeat tips and opens the way you left it

To pick the language the first time, the game reads your device's language. It does not store it or send it anywhere.

None of this contains your name, email, location or device identifiers, and the game sends none of it off your device.

System backups. The game does not exclude its data from system backups, so your save and preferences are included in them if you have them turned on:

  • iOS/iPadOS: in your iCloud or computer backup, depending on your settings.
  • Android: in Google backup (in your Google Drive account, encrypted with your device's PIN, pattern or password from Android 9 onwards) and in data transfers to a new phone.

These backups are made by the system, not by the game, and Apple and Google manage them according to your settings. We have no access to them.

4. Exporting and importing your save

In Settings → "Save" you can export your save as text and import it on another device. That text contains only the game data described in section 3.

  • When you tap "Copy", the game puts the text on the clipboard. When you tap "Paste", it reads the clipboard once to pick up the text. It never reads the clipboard at any other time.
  • Where you keep or send that text (notes, email, another app) is up to you, and from then on the terms of that service apply.
  • The text is not encrypted. It contains no personal data, but anyone who has it can load your save.

5. What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, notification or tracking (App Tracking Transparency) permissions.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising, attribution, in-app purchase or crash reporting SDKs.
  • It does not use cookies or advertising identifiers.
  • It does not sell or share data with anyone.

6. Platform services

Some features rely on services from Apple or Google. These companies process your account data as independent controllers, under their own policies. The game does not store your player ID or name in the save or anywhere else.

Game Center (iOS/iPadOS)

Optional. If you are signed in to Game Center, the game reports the achievements you earn to Apple (the achievement's identifier). If you are not signed in, the game works just the same. You can sign out in your device's Settings → Game Center. Apple's policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Google Play Games (Android)

Optional. If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game reports the achievements you earn to Google. According to Google, its SDK also collects your gamer identity (gamertag and avatar) and analytics and diagnostics data to improve the service itself, and encrypts it in transit (HTTPS). You can manage your profile's visibility or delete your games data from your Play Games profile or your Google Account. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy · Google's information on the SDK: https://developer.android.com/games/pgs/data-collection

Purchase

The App Store or Google Play handles the purchase of the game. We never receive your payment details (card, bank account or similar).

  • App Store: Apple does not give us any personal data about buyers. We only see sales reports with totals by country. Apple handles refunds (https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
  • Google Play: in Play Console, Google shows us limited details for each order: order number, date, product, price and taxes, and the country of purchase (in some cases also the region and postcode, which are used to work out taxes). If you ask us for help with a purchase, we can look up your order by its number or by your Google Account email. We only use these details to answer questions about the purchase, handle refunds and meet our tax and accounting obligations, never for advertising or to contact you unprompted. The details stay in Play Console, where Google keeps them under its policy; we only copy them elsewhere when accounting requires it (for example, sales reports), and then we keep them for as long as the law requires.

Ratings

From time to time, the game may ask the system to show the App Store's or Google Play's native rating prompt. The store decides whether to show it and handles it; the game does not know whether you saw it or what you answered.

Steam (desktop version, later)

When the game comes to Steam, it will use Steamworks for achievements and Steam Cloud to keep a copy of your save (the same content as section 3) on Valve's servers, linked to your Steam account. It will not use any other service. You can turn off Steam Cloud for the game in its properties on Steam. Valve's policy: https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/

Web version

There is no public web version of the game at the moment.

7. Legal basis

The game does not send us any personal data about you: game data stays on your device, under your control. The services in section 6 rely on your choice to use them and on the terms you accept with each platform. We only handle personal data in these cases:

  • If you email us: to reply, because you have asked us to.
  • Google Play order details: on the basis of our legitimate interest in supporting people who have bought the game, and to meet our legal tax and accounting obligations.

8. Retention and deletion

  • On your device: your save and its backups are kept until you delete them. The game has no option to delete your save: to delete everything, uninstall the app. If system backup is on, restoring your device could bring the save back; you can manage or delete those backups in your iCloud or Google backup settings.
  • On the platforms: Game Center, Google Play Games and Steam achievements, and the Steam Cloud copy, are kept by Apple, Google and Valve. You can withdraw your consent by signing out or turning the feature off, and ask for deletion with each platform's tools (see section 6).
  • Us: if you email us, we only use your address and what you tell us to reply, and we delete the messages 12 months after the last message in the conversation. Google Play order details are kept as explained in section 6.

9. Security

Your save is stored in the app's private space, which other apps cannot access. Communication with Game Center, Google Play Games and Steam is encrypted by those services.

10. Children

The game is meant for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. The game collects no personal data from anyone, children included. Game Center, Google Play Games and Steam apply their own rules to children's accounts.

11. International transfers

We receive email in a Gmail account, a Google service, and we see orders in Play Console, also run by Google. Google may process this data outside the European Economic Area under its policy and with the safeguards the law requires. Apple, Google and Valve may also process your account data outside your country under their policies.

12. Your rights

You can ask us for access to, correction or deletion of, restriction of, or object to the use of any data we hold about you by writing to jmssdeveloper@gmail.com. Since we hold hardly any data about you, there will usually be little to access, correct or delete, but we will reply within the legal time limit. If you think we have not handled your request properly, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, https://www.aepd.es) or with the data protection authority in your country.

13. Changes to this policy

If the game changes how it handles data (for example, when it comes to a new platform), we will update this policy before the change reaches the game and change the date above. If the change means collecting new data, we will explain it inside the game.
